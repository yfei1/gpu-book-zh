Details
  • Per-SM and full-GPU residency cap: in v1 (<<<2304, 128>>>), each SM holds at most 16 resident CTAs at once (all 2,048 resident threads per SM full; see Chapter 3, Section 1), giving 2,112 v1 residency slots across 132 SMs.
  • Count split across waves: splits the 2,304 CTAs by count into 2,112 in Wave 1 plus 192 in the tail (position does not indicate row order; which 192 rows land in the tail and per-CTA start/exit spread were not measured on v1).
  • Immediate slot refill: whenever a CTA finishes 64 iterations and exits, if pending CTAs remain, its freed residency slot is refilled right away.
  • Final-step tail occupancy view: the final step keeps the chain-tick 64–128 (tail interval) occupancy—about 9% (192) in use and about 91% (1,920) empty—on view for comparison.
  • Schematic and axis notes: chip, whole-HBM arrow anchor, per-SM slot placement, and the chain-tick axis are schematic, not microsecond duration.
