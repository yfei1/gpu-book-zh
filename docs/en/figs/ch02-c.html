Private caches are not coherent across streaming multiprocessors, so handoff needs paired device-wide release and acquire on the counter to guarantee partial-sum visibility by specification.

Details
  • Cross-SM merge and no co-residency: two half-row CTAs (2 of 132 SMs on the GPU, schematic positions) merge partial sums through the 60 MiB shared L2 with a thread_scope_device acq_rel atomic add; per-SM private L1 caches are not coherent across SMs, and the first arriver (old value 0) exits right after its atomic add without requiring co-residency.
  • Normative visibility guarantee: normatively, paired release and acquire on cnt[i] guarantee part[2i] visibility.
  • Observed private-cache invalidation on this GPU (not a specification guarantee): reload tests after a standalone device-wide acq_rel fence (fence.acq_rel.gpu) empirically observed that the entire private L1 is invalidated, slowing 1 CTA re-reading the 16 KiB vector xxx from 319.8 cycles with L1 valid to 596.6 cycles; reload cost after the atomic add was not measured.
  • Readback and handoff timings: reading back from L2 takes about 328 cycles or about 0.18 µs at about 1.8 GHz (partition split not measured); the wait-free merge in v3 was not measured, and a spinning handoff microbenchmark takes about 0.67 µs.
  • Pending-write wait and inferred target: with 1 pending write, a single-thread acq_rel atomic add waits 27.5–31.1 (about 30) extra cycles in measurement (1,047.2–1,050.8 → 1,078.3 cycles, about 0.60 µs; timed separately, the standalone device acq_rel fence waits +206 extra cycles, 269 → 475 cycles), whose target of waiting for prior writes to reach L2 is inferred from the memory model (not verified with hardware counters).
  • Unordered counterexample and two independent hazards: the relaxed counterexample (about 506 cycles on a single thread, 572 fewer than acq_rel) draws one specification-allowed execution (not measured on this GPU), illustrating the two independent hazards of counter-before-data at L2 and hitting an un-invalidated stale line in L1B.
  • Schematic conventions and undetermined path: the horizontal axis shows order only, with bar widths and the in-kernel earlier same-line stale L1B copy schematic; in Step 4, part[2i+1]'s read path depends on the L1 store policy, which was not determined.
